Privacy Policy

How Perminov Studios collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: December 24, 2025

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Perminov Studios ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you visit this site, browse the portfolio, or interact with features such as the gallery, contact links, or account-related areas.

We designed this experience to be simple and privacy-conscious. We only collect the minimum information needed to operate and improve the site.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

Depending on how you use the site, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Contact details you share when reaching out (e.g. email).
  • Content you submit through forms, feedback, or collaboration requests.
  • Any files or links you voluntarily provide for project inquiries.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you browse the site, certain technical data may be collected automatically, such as:

  • Basic device and browser information (e.g. screen size).
  • Rudimentary usage patterns (pages viewed, time on page).
  • Error and performance signals to keep the experience smooth.

2.3 Cookies and similar technologies

This site may use lightweight cookies or similar technologies to remember basic preferences (such as animation or motion settings). We do not use cookies for invasive tracking or third-party advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Display and maintain the portfolio and related content.
  • Respond to messages, inquiries, or collaboration requests.
  • Monitor performance, debug issues, and keep the site secure.
  • Improve design, navigation, and overall experience over time.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only in limited situations, such as:

  • Service providers that help host, maintain, or monitor the site (for example, analytics or infrastructure tools), bound by appropriate safeguards.
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect our rights and the security of this site.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. If you contact us directly, we may keep your message history to manage ongoing collaboration or support.

6. Your Choices

  • You may choose not to provide certain information; however, this may limit some features (for example, responding to a contact request).
  • You can request that certain information you have provided be updated or deleted where applicable.
  • You can configure your browser to block cookies or alert you before they are stored.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No online system can be completely secure, but we continuously aim to keep this experience safe and resilient.

8. International Visitors

This portfolio may be accessed from different regions. By using the site, you understand that information may be transferred and processed where the hosting infrastructure is located, which may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction.

9. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect design, feature, or legal changes. When we do, we will adjust the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can reach out through the contact options provided in the portfolio.
